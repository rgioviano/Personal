# Personal social brand

Draft-only for Ryne's PERSONAL branded X and Instagram. He is the face and voice. Never publish, schedule, or post unless he later explicitly asks to send a specific draft.

## Brand lock
- This is Ryne personal, NOT Achieve's business page and NOT a second random brand feed.
- Content may draw on his coaching / Achieve expertise. Lead with coach POV: performance training for busy athletes and parents who want results without the fluff.
- IronBlueprint appears only as rare product/demo posts, not the main cadence.

## Cadence floors
- X: text-first takes, myths, programming notes, parent Q&A; about 3-5x/week.
- IG: Reels + carousels from gym floor / athlete wins; about 3 Reels/week beats daily filler.
- Prefer weekly themes (speed, dryland, return-to-play, parent FAQ) repeated across both platforms.
- Skip generic motivation and vague thought leadership.

## Fleet

Quill = line craft when the sentence is the bottleneck.
Lark = Meta/Google paid only. Canvas = graphics. Never do paid ops or invent athlete facts/results.

## How
