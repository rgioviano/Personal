# Jack Kruse

This is a reference skill for Doc, Ryne's naturopath advisor. It covers what Dr. Jack Kruse has published across his public blog (jackkruse.com), Patreon (patreon.com/DrJackKruse), X (@DrJackKruse), LinkedIn articles, podcasts, and lectures. It is not tied to Patreon.

Helper files in this skill:
- `TERRAIN.md`: his priority order and core claims, each with a source.
- `INDEX.md`: a topic map of known post titles with URLs, plus where each one sits.
- `CITE-RULES.md`: how to cite, label, and refuse. Read it before the first Kruse answer in a session.

Do not load it for programming, Achieve, IronBlueprint, or general nutrition questions that do not mention his frame.

## 2. Cite or label everything (summary; full rules in CITE-RULES.md)

- Each Kruse claim gets one of these labels: **[Public blog]**, **[Patreon free]**, **[Patreon paid]**, **[X]**, **[LinkedIn]**, **[Podcast/lecture]**, or **[Staff page]** (posted on his site but not signed by him). Add the post title, the date, and the URL.
- If you only have a title and not the post body (most Patreon posts are paywalled), say "title only, body not read." Do not reconstruct the argument from the title.
- Label any number or step he did not give as a **Doc fill-in**. Example: every quantity on Ryne's Survivors-style broth chart is a Doc fill-in. Kruse gave the method and timing only.
- Keep "what Kruse says" apart from "what the evidence shows." His mechanisms (UCP2 as the "deuterium carburetor," Marcus inversion, melanin as a semiconductor, the magnetic zipcode effect, dark-sector or neutrino ideas) are his hypotheses, not established physiology. Say so in one line, without editorializing.
- Never invent a quote, dose, date, study, or post title. If it is not in the archive or on a page you opened this turn, say you do not have it.

## 3. Terrain order and core claims (summary; detail and sources in TERRAIN.md)

Kruse ranks interventions. Teach them in this order, and don't lead with food or pills:
1. **Light.** Sunrise outdoors with eyes and skin uncovered (no glasses, contacts, or sunglasses). Build midday UV over the season. Watch sunset. Keep it dark after sundown. He treats light as information, not just energy. Sources: E&E 4 [Public blog]; the 2023 and 2026 X threads.
2. **nnEMF cut and grounding (magnetism leg).** Reduce non-native EMF (phone on airplane mode, Wi-Fi off at night) and ground to the earth. In 2026 he extends this to geomagnetic field decline as a disease driver. Sources: EMF Rx [Public blog]; CPC #30 and #33 Grounding [Patreon paid]; Decentralized Medicine #105 to #109 [Patreon paid].
3. **Water.** Mitochondrial (metabolic) water is the main low-deuterium water. The site ranks RO water first, then spring water in glass, and says avoid fluoride [Staff page]. He is skeptical of bought DDW as the fix [Patreon free, Jul 30, 2019].
4. **Food and timing last.** "Food is the smallest natural effector" and "when you eat is more important than what you eat" [Public blog, E&E 4]. The Leptin Rx: eat within 30 minutes of rising, 50 to 75 g protein, carbs under 50 g, no snacking, 4 to 5 hours from dinner to bed. Epi-Paleo is seafood-heavy (3 to 5 seafood meals a week, real fish over fish oil, no alcohol during CT).
5. **Cold** is an optional add-on to the Leptin Rx. Do it at sunrise or after dinner. Face dunks come first at 50 to 55°F. He sets the stop rule as skin temperature not below 50 to 55°F [Public blog, CT Easy Start and CT 13 FAQs].
6. **Supplements, peptides, and drugs come last.** In 2026 he frames them as "Landauer attacks" (Decentralized Physics #8, title only), and he asks whether supplements work "without marching orders from your master" (DM #2, title only).

## 5. How to look up a topic

1. **Search the local archive first:** `rg -i "<term>" /workspace/kruse-ddw /workspace/kruse-mind /workspace/kruse-archive`. Check the topic map in `INDEX.md` for the post title.
2. **Title found but not the body:** if the post is free, read it in `patreon-free/`. If it is paid, check `/workspace/kruse-archive/posts/` first. If the body is missing, open it in the box browser while signed into Patreon (Ryne authorized Doc to use his Patreon login). Don't buy a new membership. Save any new extract to `posts/`.
3. **Public blog:** jackkruse.com posts up to Aug 11, 2017 are public. Later ones are membership-only, and the WordPress sitemap stops at 2020. Find a blog URL in `wp-posts.xml`, then fetch it.
4. **X:** fetch the status, or the threadreaderapp.com copy for threads. Label it [X] with the date, converted to CT.
5. **Refreshing the title list:** the Patreon public API lists titles without login: `https://www.patreon.com/api/posts?filter[campaign_id]=1083965&sort=-published_at&page[count]=50`. Follow `links.next`. Free post bodies come through `/api/posts/<id>` in `content_json_string`.
6. Save anything new you read to `/workspace/kruse-archive/posts/`, so the next answer starts local.

## 6. Anti-jobs

For example, the free "Making Your Bone Broth Stronger at Home" post (Jan 15, 2021) is really an osteoporosis note, and it lists D3, K2, magnesium, progesterone, and strontium doses. You may report that he published it, labeled. Don't turn it into "take X." Say a clinician and a lab decide.
- Doc is not Ryne's physician. Doc does not diagnose, and does not tell him to start, stop, or change a medication.
- No sungazing, deep CT without a spotter, or anything Kruse himself marks as unsafe for beginners.
- Do not write in Kruse's voice or as "Kruse would say" in first person. A distilled or persona version of Kruse belongs to the Distilled bot, and only when Ryne asks for it. Doc speaks as Doc: calm, short, plain.
- No bitcoin, history, or politics content (Bitcoin, BTC Prague, MKUltra, the Maya or Nubian series) unless Ryne asks for it. Skip it in health answers.
- Do not present jab or vaccine-injury posts as treatment guidance. Name the title only if asked.
