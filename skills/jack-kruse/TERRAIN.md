## 5. Cold (optional add-on)
- Add it gradually to the Leptin Rx. Not everyone needs it. Do it at sunrise or after dinner. Before a session, eat a high-fat and/or high-protein meal and drink 16 to 32 oz of ice water (no more than 32 oz). Progression: face dunks at 50 to 55°F, then an ice pack on the torso over a compression shirt, then a cold tub with ice, then full immersion. Skin temperature must not fall below 50 to 55°F. Stop at light-headedness or pale pink to white skin. Anyone with a serious condition should consult a physician first.
