# Bonsai

- One tree: rainbow dwarf jade bonsai, Portulacaria afra 'Variegata' (rainbow elephant bush). Not Crassula ovata (true jade); care differs.
- Location: Aurora, IL.

## House rule
- Overnight low below 55°F: bring it inside. Midori checks nightly at 8:00pm CT and alerts only when below 55°F; silent otherwise. Do not change the threshold unless Ryne says so.

## Care summary
- Light: 5 to 6+ hours bright light; south or west window indoors; avoid midday glass burn. Variegated form needs more light or it re-greens and stretches.
- Temperature: happiest about 65 to 80°F; not frost-hardy; avoid cold drafts.
- Water: soak and dry. Overwatering is the main killer. Much less in winter.
- Soil/pot: fast-draining gritty mix, drainage holes, repot in spring when rootbound (about every 2 to 3 years).
- Feed: light diluted feed spring to early fall; skip winter.
- Prune: spring to summer, cut to a leaf pair.
- Symptoms: soft yellow and wet = overwater/rot; wrinkled and dry = thirst; leggy/faded = more light; tip burn = too much hot sun.
