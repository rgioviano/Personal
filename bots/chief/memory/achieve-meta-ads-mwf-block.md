## How briefs get tasks
1. **Month packet (preferred):** Marketing’s routine fires **1st of each month**, builds month-specific M/W/F 45–60 min checklists, and hands them to CoS. CoS puts that month’s Mon/Wed/Fri lines on morning priorities + evening wrap (short Meta bullets only on M/W/F).
2. **Current packet:** `achieve-meta-ads-current-month.md` = **MONTH 01 October 2026** (ACTIVE 2026-10-01).
3. **Reference cards:** `achieve-meta-ads-mwf-by-month.md` (full year month cards).

Do **not** invent a generic year-round task list when a current-month packet exists. Sep pre-launch is over.

## Standing constraints (all months)
- 45–60 minutes, **Mon / Wed / Fri only**
- Never: boost posts · change budget >20% inside 72h · sell adults after 10:00 a.m. · start in-season HS girls on Build before Nov 16 (Foundation only through Nov 14) · blow monthly budget in 10 days · daily $16.40
